Overview & Production Lore

Released on 08 September 2025 via Liquid Len Media as a focus single from debut solo album The Enemy Is Us, 'Almost' examines the gut-wrenching feeling of standing on the brink of success only to watch it slip through your fingers.

Pairing minimalist electronic textures with electric guitar contributions from History Of Guns collaborator Caden Clarkson, the track takes a candid look at best-laid plans—across love, career, and life—and the raw moment when hope gives way to anger, self-blame, and quiet defeat.
