Max Rael

Solo Works

Bringing together unique sound designs and outsider spoken-word perspectives on a world in freefall. Fusing minimalist synthpop, darkwave, and loud drum arrangements, Rael's solo catalogue explores themes of existential confrontation, neurodivergent identity, loss, and resilience.

The Enemy Is Us 2025
Liquid Len Recording Company (20 June 2025)

Debut solo album. A 12-track dark bouquet of minimalist synth, darkwave, and spoken-word electronic pop. Evolving out of earlier instrumental Raelism EPs (2014–2015), the record confronts a precarious world with rhythmic energy and an unyielding core of hope.
